Configure

Open the player and click Settings. Pitch and EQ aren't heard here.

Settings

Saved in config/cuemymusic/config.json, with your likes, dislikes and playlists. Delete it to reset.

Setting Default Notes
Pause during travel Off Fades out at a portal, then back in once the new dimension loads.
Crossfade 4 s Songs need the same sounds.json volume and sample rate.
Automix add-on songs On Matches loudness and blends add-on songs into each other (see Mixing).
Keep add-on songs out of recordings On Hides add-on songs from game-audio recorders, not from recorders of all system audio.

Add-on song analysis is cached in config/cuemymusic/analysis/. It's safe to delete.
