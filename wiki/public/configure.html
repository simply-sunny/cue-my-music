Configure

Open the player and click Settings. Pitch and EQ aren't heard here.

The interactive player needs JavaScript.

What the screen leaves out:

Setting Notes
Pause during travel Fades out at a portal, then back in once the new dimension loads.
Crossfade Songs need the same sounds.json volume and sample rate.
Automix add-on songs On. Matches loudness and blends add-on songs into each other (see Mixing).
Keep add-on songs out of recordings On. Hides add-on songs from game-audio recorders, not from recorders of all system audio.

Settings, likes, dislikes and playlists are saved in config/cuemymusic.json. Delete it to reset.

Add-on song analysis is cached in config/cuemymusic-analysis/. It's safe to delete.

The JSON goes further than the screen. Restart after editing:

Key Default Notes
speed 1.0 0.5 to 2.0, to two decimals.
