Cue My Music

A music player for Minecraft's soundtrack, with a queue, pitch, speed and an equalizer.

Browse every song by artist or resource pack, and queue up what's next. When a list ends, similar songs carry on, or the game takes over. Only background music changes. Jukeboxes and sound effects don't.
